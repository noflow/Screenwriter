Quest & event builder

Build the arc first, then write each objective as a quest stage. This keeps story progression, rewards, and follow-up events visible in one place.

1Define the arc

Example: Emma asks the player to help prepare for her first day at a new job.

2Add objectives

Talk to Emma → buy interview clothes → meet her at the café.

3Add rewards

Emma Trust +3, Emma Love +1, and Player Confidence +2 can all be separate reward rows.

4Schedule a follow-up

Use a game date such as Y1-08-23, then choose a time and location.

Tip: choose how each objective finishes inside its quest stage—for example, after a conversation, when an item is obtained, or when a stat reaches a value.
